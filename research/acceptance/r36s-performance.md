# R36S performance: benchmark, the 60 fps result and the quality budget

**Status: the 60 fps gate is MET at the current defaults on the R36S** (640x480, VSync on,
Smooth motion on, MSAA 4x, anisotropy 8x, render scale 1.0, all enhancements off). The
benchmark level, level 1-1 and level 3-10 all hold 60, and Smooth motion off is the same.

## Device and build

- R36S: aarch64, kernel 4.4.189, 640x480 60.02 Hz panel, Mali-G31, OpenGL ES 3.2
  (`v1.r13p0`), 895 MB RAM.
- Build: `tools/r36s/build.sh`, Debian 13 arm64 container, static SDL3 3.4.18.
- EmulationStation was stopped for each run and started again afterwards; the game ran on
  KMSDRM. GPU governor `simple_ondemand` (400 MHz); forcing `performance` (520 MHz) made no
  difference.
- The benchmark level is generated by `src/core/benchmark_level.cpp`: 40 rows, 287 + 64 level
  quads, **35 enemies of 12 kinds**, 6 coins, 5 torches, 3 fences, 4 platforms, 5 props, 1 candle
  row and 2 water rows. It is valid, `mbn_benchmark_tests` builds its geometry without
  `MageBros.exe`, and it never appears in the campaign.

All runs meet the target: average >= 59, 1% low >= 55, no sustained 30 fps. The longest frame in
a run is about 33 ms (one occasional page-flip hitch), but it is below 1 % of the samples; the
p99 stays at 16.8 ms. Smooth motion on does not lower the rate (it is the same as off).

## What made the difference

The starting point (old build, same benchmark) was 11.8 fps / 41 ms CPU / 366 draws / 1.16 MB
uploaded a frame. The final build is 59.9 fps / 6.6 ms CPU / 90 draws / 11 KB. In order:

1. **State cache in `Device`**: unchanged framebuffer, viewport, program, depth/blend/cull,
   matrices, material, light, texture stage, texture binding/filtering and replay uniforms are
   sent only when they change.
2. **Static mesh buffers**: models and the level's floor/underground/goal keep their vertices on
   the card; uploads fall to the frame's dynamic quads only.
3. **Opaque and alpha model materials merged**: one draw per model, with a per-vertex material
   slot and the material table as uniforms. The triangle order is unchanged, so blending and
   depth order stay the same. Draws fell from 366 to 226; the level-viewer screenshot is byte
   identical to 0.4.47.
4. **Direct window MSAA on GLES**: when the window covers the picture, render scale is 1, Bloom
   is off and Brightness is 100, the game draws into the window's own multisampled buffer; the
   display resolves it in the swap instead of `glBlitFramebuffer` into an off-screen texture.
5. **A dedicated level/flat-sprite shader**: the level's pre-lit point-sampled meshes and the
   game's flat rectangles use one `texture * vertexColour` shader instead of the full
   AO/shadow/light/bloom shader. This was the decisive fill win: 1-1/3-10 went from 30 to 60 at
   640x480. The shader keeps the same point/linear, mipmap and anisotropy filtering as before and
   is only selected when the draw can be expressed exactly that way.
6. **2D/HUD batching**: HUD and other 2D quads are gathered by texture/state and drawn in order
   in far fewer calls; CPU fell by about 1.8 ms and the frame has headroom for the occasional
   spike.
7. **A replay VAO fix**: dynamic shadows/particles were setting their vertex layout on the last
   model's VAO, so the next instance of that model could disappear while its shadow stayed. The
   replay now binds the main VAO for every dynamic draw. This bug made the first benchmark runs
   faster by omitting objects; the numbers above are from the corrected build.
8. **Benchmark density calibrated to the busiest real level**: the first draft (63 enemies,
   19 candle rows, more props) was heavier than 3-10 and could not hold 60. The final level keeps
   the variety and many mobs but about the visible load of 3-10.

## Why it was 30 or 60, never 45

The KMSDRM panel flips at 60 Hz and SDL's swap waits for a page flip; there is no
show-immediately path. A frame that is not ready before the next refresh waits a whole 16.7 ms,
so the stable rates are 60, 30, 20... (VSync on/off measured identically on the console). At the
old build, 100 % of frames were about 33 ms; after the level shader and batching, 99.8 % are
under 20 ms and the occasional miss costs one 33 ms frame.

The investigation that found the fill limit is kept in the history of this change: skipping the
level meshes or the flat sprites individually still locked to 30; skipping both reached 60;
texture sampling of the 256x256 level texture was the largest single cost; MSAA 0/2/4, texture
mipmaps, vertex colours, depth test/write, backface culling and the GPU clock were ruled out.

## Quality budget for the next iteration

Same benchmark, 6-second runs, one setting changed at a time (default 59.68 average in this
series):

| setting | average FPS | 1% low | p95 | note |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| current defaults (MSAA 4x, aniso 8x) | 59.68 | 59.35 | 16.76 | reference |
| Ambient occlusion On | 58.69 | 30.02 | 16.78 | borderline, above 1 % misses |
| Dynamic shadows Low | 59.52 | 59.58 | 16.76 | fits in 60 |
| Dynamic lights On | 30.01 | 29.86 | 33.42 | needs work |
| Bloom Low | 15.00 | 14.98 | 66.72 | needs work |
| MSAA 0 | 59.68 | 59.39 | 16.73 | no material gain |
| anisotropy 1 | 59.68 | 59.54 | 16.72 | no material gain |

The next iteration can start from AO and shadows (both already hold 60) and must reduce the cost
of dynamic lights and bloom, or keep them as quality-only options, before considering them at
the default.

## Verification

- `ctest` on macOS: 8/8; in the R36S Debian 13 container: 5/5.
- Level-viewer screenshot byte identical to the 0.4.47 build (`VIEWER-SAME`); the HUD's bottom
  strip is byte identical (`HUD-BOT-SAME`). The Saito logo, the title screen and level 1-1 were
  checked on the R36S itself after the 2D and VAO fixes; the title at 20 s is byte identical to
  0.4.47.
- The benchmark writes `benchmark-report.json` and `benchmark.log`; `MageBrosBench.sh` runs it
  from the Ports menu or over SSH (it stops/restarts EmulationStation when needed). The final
  launcher run on the console measured 59.85 fps average, 1% low 59.39, p99 16.84 ms.
- The console was left with MSAA 4x, anisotropy 8x, render scale 1.0, Smooth motion on, all
  enhancements off. EmulationStation is running.

## Reproduce

```sh
tools/r36s/build.sh
tools/r36s/install.sh ark@192.168.0.104
ssh ark@192.168.0.104 /roms2/ports/MageBrosBench.sh      # 30 s benchmark + report
```

Or on the desktop:

```sh
./MageBrosNative --game /path/to/MageBros.exe --benchmark --benchmark-seconds 30 \
    --benchmark-report reports/run.json
./MageBrosNative --game /path/to/MageBros.exe --benchmark --benchmark-level 1,1 --benchmark-seconds 30
```
